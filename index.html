Recent projects

Memory-Intensive Tasks with Test-Time Training

Leann Alhashishi, Sebastian W. Pattinson, Christos Margadji.

ICRA 2027 (submitted). [PDF]

Fit per-episode neural implicit representations over robot video via MAML, then projected the weights as tokens into TinyLlama-1.1B (QA) and Pythia-400M (action control). No raw frames at inference: +19.1pp over baseline on memory-dependent video QA, and R² +0.51-0.54 on held-out joint-action regression.

InLink

Leann Alhashishi, Ahmed Haytham.

Building a weight-space implicit memory model as a RAG alternative for enterprise QA. 100x fewer context tokens and 55x faster retrieval than dense retrieval, over 5K synthetic documents.

Upper-Limb Synergy Modeling from Real-World Contactless Motion Tracking

Parthan Olikkal, Leann Alhashishi, Rajasekhar Anguluri, Ramana Vinjamuri.

BIOROB 2026 (accepted, oral). [PDF]

Reduced synergy dimensionality by ~50% (7-10 to 3-5 dimensions) and captured ~10% more variance than PCA (p < 1e-7 across 40 subjects) using Robust PCA on noisy vision-tracked hand motion, validated on a 6-DoF robotic hand.
